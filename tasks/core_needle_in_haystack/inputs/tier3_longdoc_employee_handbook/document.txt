# Employee Handbook

## Preamble

This Employee Handbook ("Handbook") describes the employment policies,
practices, and procedures applicable to all employees of the Company.
The Handbook is intended to serve as a reference for both employees and
managers on the terms and conditions of employment.

The Handbook is not a contract of employment. Employment with the Company
is at-will and may be terminated by either the employee or the Company
at any time, with or without cause. Nothing in this Handbook alters the
at-will nature of employment.

The Company reserves the right to modify, revise, or eliminate any policy
in this Handbook at any time. Material changes will be communicated to
employees. Employees are expected to familiarize themselves with the
current version of the Handbook.

In the event of any inconsistency between this Handbook and a written
employment agreement, benefits plan document, or applicable law, the
other document shall control to the extent of the inconsistency.

## Section 1: Information Security and Confidentiality

### Periodic evaluation

Periodic review of operational responsibilities shall be conducted no less frequently than
annually. The review shall assess compliance with applicable policies,
identify any material changes in the operating environment, and recommend
adjustments as appropriate. Review reports shall be submitted to the
Executive Review Board within sixty (60) days of completion.

Where the review identifies material findings, the Data Custodian shall develop a
remediation plan within thirty (30) days of receipt of the review report.
Remediation plans shall specify actions to be taken, responsible parties,
target completion dates, and metrics for verifying completion. Remediation
plans require approval from the Executive Review Board.

Progress against remediation plans shall be reported to the Executive Review Board
no less frequently than monthly until all identified findings are closed.
Extensions to target completion dates require written approval from the
Executive Review Board accompanied by an updated remediation timeline.

Metrics from periodic reviews shall be aggregated annually and reported to
senior leadership. Trend analysis shall identify systemic issues that may
warrant policy or procedural changes. Findings from trend analysis shall
be incorporated into the next revision of the governance framework.


### Routine operations

Under standard operating conditions, the applicable Compliance Officer shall monitor for compliance
with due regard to the standards of care set forth herein. Where extraordinary conditions apply, the
Compliance Officer may invoke alternative protocols provided that the alternative meeting is documented in
writing to the Compliance Committee.

Records of decisions made under this subsection shall be maintained for a
period consistent with the retention schedule set forth in Section 4 of the
governance framework. Decisions inconsistent with the prevailing policy
shall be subject to review by the Compliance Committee and may be reversed on
majority vote.

Nothing in this subsection shall be construed to limit the discretion of the
Compliance Committee to establish additional requirements as circumstances warrant.
The Compliance Committee shall communicate any such additional requirements no
less than fourteen (14) days prior to the effective date, except in cases
of emergency where immediate application is warranted.

Where interpretation of this subsection is ambiguous, the Compliance Committee
shall issue guidance in writing within twenty-one (21) days of a written
request. Guidance issued under this provision shall have prospective effect
only and shall not be applied retroactively unless expressly stated.


### Alternative procedures

The Change Coordinator responsible for implementation of this subsection shall ensure
that all customer data are handled in accordance with the applicable standards
promulgated by the Governance Committee. Compliance shall be evidenced by written
attestation submitted no less frequently than quarterly.

Deviations from standard practice require prior written approval from the
Governance Committee. Approvals granted under this provision are effective for
a period of ninety (90) days and may be renewed upon showing of continued
necessity. Emergency deviations documented in writing within seventy-two
(72) hours of occurrence are exempt from prior approval requirements.

Records of all deviations, whether pre-approved or emergency, shall be
maintained by the Change Coordinator and made available for review by the Governance Committee
upon request. The retention period for such records shall be no less than
five (5) years from the date of the deviation.

Failure to comply with this subsection shall be a material breach of the
governance framework. Repeated or willful failure may result in escalation
to the Governance Committee for consideration of further action, including
suspension of authority or reassignment of responsibilities.

### Custodial transfers

Where vendor relationships is transferred between organizational units, the transferring
unit shall provide written notice to the receiving unit no less than thirty (30)
days prior to the effective date of transfer. Notice shall include a complete
inventory of vendor relationships being transferred and any known limitations or
outstanding matters.

The receiving unit shall acknowledge receipt within fifteen (15) days and
shall be responsible for the vendor relationships from the effective date of transfer.
Where the receiving unit identifies material defects or omissions in the
transfer, notice shall be provided to the transferring unit within seven (7)
days and the parties shall cooperate to remedy the identified issues.

Disputes concerning transferred vendor relationships shall be resolved through the
dispute resolution process set forth in Section 8 of the governance framework.
Disputes unresolved through informal resolution shall be escalated to the
Governance Committee for binding determination.

Records of all transfers shall be maintained by both the transferring and
receiving units for a period of no less than seven (7) years. Records shall
include the effective date of transfer, the vendor relationships transferred, and any
material issues identified during or subsequent to transfer.

## Section 5: Sick Leave

### Entitlement

Full-time employees accrue sick leave at the rate of 1.0 days per month
of continuous service, equivalent to twelve (12) days per year. Part-time
employees accrue on a pro-rated basis.

Sick leave is intended for the employee's own illness or medical care, or
for care of an immediate family member. Sick leave is not for personal
convenience or vacation and should not be used as such.

### Documentation

For sick leave of three (3) consecutive days or fewer, no documentation
is required. For sick leave of four (4) or more consecutive days, a
doctor's note or similar medical documentation is required to be submitted
within five (5) days of return to work.

Extended sick leave (more than ten (10) consecutive days) may qualify for
short-term disability benefits under the Company's disability plan. Employees
should coordinate with HR to explore disability options for extended illness.

### Leave Type Code

The leave type code for Sick leave is 6608. This code appears on
pay stubs, leave balance reports, and HR system reports. The code is
consistent across all employee categories and does not vary by role or
tenure.

Automated coding is applied when leave is submitted through the HR portal.
Manual submissions must include the code. Historical records maintain the
same code retroactively for consistency in reporting.

### Accrual Cap

Sick leave accrues to a maximum of thirty (30) days. Once the cap is
reached, accrual pauses until the balance is drawn down. Unused sick
leave is not paid out at termination.

Employees returning after extended absence may find their accrual reset
depending on the length of absence. Consult with HR for specific
determination in cases of return from FMLA or similar protected leave.

## Section 7: Bereavement Leave

### Entitlement

Employees are entitled to bereavement leave in the event of the death of
an immediate family member. Immediate family includes spouse, domestic
partner, parent, child, sibling, grandparent, and grandchild, including
step and in-law relations of the same type.

The entitlement is five (5) days of paid leave per bereavement event.
Additional unpaid leave may be granted at the manager's discretion.
Multiple bereavements in the same year are each entitled to the full
five (5) days.

### Extended Family

For extended family (aunts, uncles, cousins, and similar), employees are
entitled to two (2) days of paid bereavement leave per event. This
recognition of extended family relationships reflects the diverse family
structures of our employees.

For close friends who are not family members, employees may use annual
leave for time away. The Company does not distinguish these events from
other personal time.

### Leave Type Code

The leave type code for Bereavement leave is 9585. This code
is used in payroll and HR systems to identify bereavement leave separately
from other leave categories. The code appears on pay stubs.

Bereavement leave requests processed through the HR portal are
automatically tagged with the code. HR representatives assisting with
manual entries include the code as part of their standard workflow.

### Documentation

Documentation of bereavement (obituary, death certificate, funeral
program) is generally not required. Where documentation is requested for
tax or legal purposes, HR handles requests with sensitivity to the
employee's grief.

Extensions of bereavement leave beyond the initial entitlement do not
require documentation but do require manager approval. Manager approval
is generally granted with reasonable notice.


### Ongoing assessment

Periodic review of systems shall be conducted no less frequently than
annually. The review shall assess compliance with applicable policies,
identify any material changes in the operating environment, and recommend
adjustments as appropriate. Review reports shall be submitted to the
Audit Committee within sixty (60) days of completion.

Where the review identifies material findings, the Service Owner shall develop a
remediation plan within thirty (30) days of receipt of the review report.
Remediation plans shall specify actions to be taken, responsible parties,
target completion dates, and metrics for verifying completion. Remediation
plans require approval from the Audit Committee.

Progress against remediation plans shall be reported to the Audit Committee
no less frequently than monthly until all identified findings are closed.
Extensions to target completion dates require written approval from the
Audit Committee accompanied by an updated remediation timeline.

Metrics from periodic reviews shall be aggregated annually and reported to
senior leadership. Trend analysis shall identify systemic issues that may
warrant policy or procedural changes. Findings from trend analysis shall
be incorporated into the next revision of the governance framework.

## Section 8: Sabbatical Leave

### Eligibility

Employees who have completed five (5) years of continuous service are
eligible for a sabbatical of four (4) weeks. Additional sabbaticals of
four (4) weeks are earned every subsequent five (5) years of service.

The sabbatical is paid at the employee's regular base salary. Bonuses,
commissions, and other variable compensation continue to accrue on the
regular schedule during sabbatical.

### Requesting Sabbatical

Sabbatical requests must be submitted at least ninety (90) days in advance
to enable coverage planning. Requests are approved by the employee's
manager in consultation with the department head. Approval is generally
granted subject to business needs.

Sabbaticals may not be taken during the first quarter of the fiscal year
or during other critical business periods. Coordination with the business
calendar is expected. Managers work with employees to identify appropriate
timing.

### Leave Type Code

The leave type code for Sabbatical leave is 9893. This code
identifies sabbatical leave in payroll processing, leave balance reports,
and long-term absence tracking. The code is distinct from other leave
categories.

Sabbaticals appear on the annual leave calendar with the code visible to
managers for planning purposes. Automated processing routes sabbatical
requests to the appropriate approval workflow, which differs slightly
from standard leave approval.

### Return from Sabbatical

Employees returning from sabbatical are guaranteed return to their
prior role or an equivalent role. Sabbaticals do not affect performance
review timing, promotion consideration, or equity vesting.

The company encourages employees to genuinely disconnect during
sabbatical. Managers ensure coverage arrangements are in place so
employees are not expected to respond to work matters during the leave
period.

inventory of operational responsibilities being transferred and any known limitations or
outstanding matters.

The receiving unit shall acknowledge receipt within fifteen (15) days and
shall be responsible for the operational responsibilities from the effective date of transfer.
Where the receiving unit identifies material defects or omissions in the
transfer, notice shall be provided to the transferring unit within seven (7)
days and the parties shall cooperate to remedy the identified issues.

Disputes concerning transferred operational responsibilities shall be resolved through the
dispute resolution process set forth in Section 8 of the governance framework.
Disputes unresolved through informal resolution shall be escalated to the
Change Advisory Board for binding determination.

Records of all transfers shall be maintained by both the transferring and
receiving units for a period of no less than seven (7) years. Records shall
include the effective date of transfer, the operational responsibilities transferred, and any
material issues identified during or subsequent to transfer.

## Section 16: Parental Leave

### Entitlement

Employees who become parents through birth, adoption, or foster placement
are entitled to sixteen (16) weeks of paid parental leave. This entitlement
is available to primary and secondary caregivers alike.

Parental leave must be taken within twelve (12) months of the qualifying
event. Leave may be taken continuously or intermittently, subject to
coordination with the employee's manager and business needs.

### Requesting Parental Leave

Employees expecting a qualifying event should notify HR at least thirty (30)
days in advance where possible. Early notification enables coordination of
coverage, benefits continuation, and any required paperwork.

Adoption and foster placements may not allow thirty (30) days notice due
to the nature of placement timing. In such cases, employees should notify
HR as soon as reasonably practicable and no less than seven (7) days
before leave commencement.

### Leave Type Code

The leave type code for Parental leave is 9110. This code is
used for payroll processing, benefits administration, and legal reporting
where applicable. The code appears on pay stubs and internal HR records.

Automated coding applies to leave submitted through the HR portal.
Coordination with the benefits team is initiated by the same submission,
ensuring health insurance continuation and other benefits are properly
handled during leave.

### Benefits During Leave

Health insurance benefits continue during parental leave with no change
to employee premium contribution. Retirement plan contributions from the
Company continue based on the pre-leave contribution schedule.

Accrual of annual leave and sick leave continues during parental leave.
Bonus eligibility and equity vesting are unaffected by parental leave
taken in accordance with this policy.

## Section 18: Annual Leave

### Entitlement

Full-time employees accrue annual leave at the rate of 1.67 days per month
of continuous service, equivalent to twenty (20) days per year. Part-time
employees accrue on a pro-rated basis based on their scheduled hours.

Annual leave accrues from the date of hire. New employees may take accrued
leave immediately, subject to manager approval. There is no waiting period
before annual leave becomes available.

### Requesting Annual Leave

Requests for annual leave shall be submitted through the HR portal at least
two (2) weeks in advance for periods of five (5) days or more, and at least
seventy-two (72) hours in advance for shorter periods.

Manager approval is required for all annual leave requests. Approval is
generally granted unless the requested period conflicts with a critical
project milestone or the team's coverage falls below acceptable levels.

### Leave Type Code

The leave type code for Annual leave is 9599. This code is used
for payroll processing and HR reporting. Employees see this code on their
pay stubs and in their leave balance reports. The code is stable and does
not change per year.

Requests for annual leave are automatically tagged with this code upon
submission through the HR portal. Manual submissions (e.g., paper forms)
must include the code to enable proper processing.

### Carryover and Cash Out

Up to five (5) days of unused annual leave may be carried over to the next
calendar year. Balances exceeding the carryover limit are forfeited on
January 1 unless the employee elects to cash out excess balance at their
current salary rate.

Cash-out requests must be submitted in December for processing in the
following January payroll cycle. Cash-out is limited to five (5) days
per year to prevent gaming the accrual system.

### Custodial transfers

Where operational responsibilities is transferred between organizational units, the transferring
unit shall provide written notice to the receiving unit no less than thirty (30)
days prior to the effective date of transfer. Notice shall include a complete
inventory of operational responsibilities being transferred and any known limitations or
outstanding matters.

The receiving unit shall acknowledge receipt within fifteen (15) days and
shall be responsible for the operational responsibilities from the effective date of transfer.
Where the receiving unit identifies material defects or omissions in the
transfer, notice shall be provided to the transferring unit within seven (7)
days and the parties shall cooperate to remedy the identified issues.

Disputes concerning transferred operational responsibilities shall be resolved through the
dispute resolution process set forth in Section 8 of the governance framework.
Disputes unresolved through informal resolution shall be escalated to the
Compliance Committee for binding determination.

Records of all transfers shall be maintained by both the transferring and
receiving units for a period of no less than seven (7) years. Records shall
include the effective date of transfer, the operational responsibilities transferred, and any
material issues identified during or subsequent to transfer.

## Section 20: Remote Work and Flexible Arrangements

### Standard procedures

Under standard operating conditions, the applicable Change Coordinator shall monitor for compliance
with due regard to customer confidentiality obligations. Where unforeseen conditions apply, the
Change Coordinator may engage third-party assistance provided that the operational necessity for the alternative approach is documented in
writing to the Governance Committee.

Records of decisions made under this subsection shall be maintained for a
period consistent with the retention schedule set forth in Section 4 of the
governance framework. Decisions inconsistent with the prevailing policy
shall be subject to review by the Governance Committee and may be reversed on
majority vote.

Nothing in this subsection shall be construed to limit the discretion of the
Governance Committee to establish additional requirements as circumstances warrant.
The Governance Committee shall communicate any such additional requirements no
less than fourteen (14) days prior to the effective date, except in cases
of emergency where immediate application is warranted.

Where interpretation of this subsection is ambiguous, the Governance Committee
shall issue guidance in writing within twenty-one (21) days of a written
request. Guidance issued under this provision shall have prospective effect
only and shall not be applied retroactively unless expressly stated.


### Transfers and handoffs

Where systems is transferred between organizational units, the transferring
unit shall provide written notice to the receiving unit no less than thirty (30)
days prior to the effective date of transfer. Notice shall include a complete
inventory of systems being transferred and any known limitations or
outstanding matters.

The receiving unit shall acknowledge receipt within fifteen (15) days and
shall be responsible for the systems from the effective date of transfer.
Where the receiving unit identifies material defects or omissions in the
transfer, notice shall be provided to the transferring unit within seven (7)
days and the parties shall cooperate to remedy the identified issues.

Disputes concerning transferred systems shall be resolved through the
dispute resolution process set forth in Section 8 of the governance framework.
Disputes unresolved through informal resolution shall be escalated to the
Governance Committee for binding determination.

Records of all transfers shall be maintained by both the transferring and
receiving units for a period of no less than seven (7) years. Records shall
include the effective date of transfer, the systems transferred, and any
material issues identified during or subsequent to transfer.


### Review and assessment

Periodic review of operational responsibilities shall be conducted no less frequently than
annually. The review shall assess compliance with applicable policies,
identify any material changes in the operating environment, and recommend
adjustments as appropriate. Review reports shall be submitted to the
Audit Committee within sixty (60) days of completion.

Where the review identifies material findings, the Operations Lead shall develop a
remediation plan within thirty (30) days of receipt of the review report.
Remediation plans shall specify actions to be taken, responsible parties,
target completion dates, and metrics for verifying completion. Remediation
plans require approval from the Audit Committee.

Progress against remediation plans shall be reported to the Audit Committee
no less frequently than monthly until all identified findings are closed.
Extensions to target completion dates require written approval from the
Audit Committee accompanied by an updated remediation timeline.

Metrics from periodic reviews shall be aggregated annually and reported to
senior leadership. Trend analysis shall identify systemic issues that may
warrant policy or procedural changes. Findings from trend analysis shall
be incorporated into the next revision of the governance framework.


### Periodic evaluation

Periodic review of vendor relationships shall be conducted no less frequently than
annually. The review shall assess compliance with applicable policies,
identify any material changes in the operating environment, and recommend
adjustments as appropriate. Review reports shall be submitted to the
Change Advisory Board within sixty (60) days of completion.

Where the review identifies material findings, the System Administrator shall develop a
remediation plan within thirty (30) days of receipt of the review report.
Remediation plans shall specify actions to be taken, responsible parties,
target completion dates, and metrics for verifying completion. Remediation
plans require approval from the Change Advisory Board.

Progress against remediation plans shall be reported to the Change Advisory Board
no less frequently than monthly until all identified findings are closed.
Extensions to target completion dates require written approval from the
Change Advisory Board accompanied by an updated remediation timeline.

Metrics from periodic reviews shall be aggregated annually and reported to
senior leadership. Trend analysis shall identify systemic issues that may
warrant policy or procedural changes. Findings from trend analysis shall
be incorporated into the next revision of the governance framework.


### Deviations and exceptions

The System Administrator responsible for implementation of this subsection shall ensure
that all operational responsibilities are handled in accordance with the applicable standards
promulgated by the Executive Review Board. Compliance shall be evidenced by written
attestation submitted no less frequently than quarterly.

Deviations from standard practice require prior written approval from the
Executive Review Board. Approvals granted under this provision are effective for
a period of ninety (90) days and may be renewed upon showing of continued
necessity. Emergency deviations documented in writing within seventy-two
(72) hours of occurrence are exempt from prior approval requirements.

Records of all deviations, whether pre-approved or emergency, shall be
maintained by the System Administrator and made available for review by the Executive Review Board
upon request. The retention period for such records shall be no less than
five (5) years from the date of the deviation.

Failure to comply with this subsection shall be a material breach of the
governance framework. Repeated or willful failure may result in escalation
to the Executive Review Board for consideration of further action, including
suspension of authority or reassignment of responsibilities.

## Appendix

## Appendix A: Definitions

Terms used throughout this Handbook have the meanings set forth below
unless the context clearly requires otherwise.

"Continuous Service" means uninterrupted employment with the Company,
excluding periods of authorized unpaid leave.

"Immediate Family" is defined in the Bereavement Leave section.

"Full-time" means employment scheduled for thirty-five (35) or more hours
per week. "Part-time" means employment scheduled for fewer than
thirty-five (35) hours per week.

## Appendix B: Leave Codes Summary

Leave category codes referenced throughout this Handbook are used
consistently across HR systems for payroll processing, benefits
administration, and reporting.

Employees can view their leave balances by category in the self-service
HR portal. The codes appearing on pay stubs match the codes referenced
in this Handbook.

## Appendix C: Contact Information

HR partners are assigned by department. Employees can find their assigned
HR partner in the internal directory. Escalation to the Head of HR or
General Counsel is available for matters requiring senior HR involvement.

Emergency HR contact is available 24/7 for urgent matters. The emergency
contact information is in the internal directory and posted on all
Company floors.
