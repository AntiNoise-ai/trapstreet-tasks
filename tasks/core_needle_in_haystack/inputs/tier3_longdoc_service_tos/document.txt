# Terms of Service

## Preamble and Interpretation

These Terms of Service ("Terms") govern the relationship between the Service
Provider and the Customer with respect to the Service. Capitalized terms not
otherwise defined shall have the meaning ascribed to them in the Master
Services Agreement between the parties.

The Terms are effective as of the Effective Date and shall remain in effect
until terminated in accordance with Section 5 (Termination). Amendments to
the Terms require written agreement of both parties, except as otherwise
provided in Section 12 (Amendments).

In the event of conflict between the Terms and the Master Services Agreement,
the Master Services Agreement shall control. In the event of conflict
between the Terms and any Order Form, the Order Form shall control with
respect to the specific matters addressed therein.

The Terms are intended to be interpreted and applied in a manner consistent
with the good faith obligations of the parties. Ambiguities shall not be
construed against the drafter. Section headings are for convenience only
and shall not affect interpretation.

## Section 1: Fees and Payment Terms

### Deviations and exceptions

The System Administrator responsible for implementation of this subsection shall ensure
that all personnel authority are handled in accordance with the applicable standards
promulgated by the Compliance Committee. Compliance shall be evidenced by written
attestation submitted no less frequently than quarterly.

Deviations from standard practice require prior written approval from the
Compliance Committee. Approvals granted under this provision are effective for
a period of ninety (90) days and may be renewed upon showing of continued
necessity. Emergency deviations documented in writing within seventy-two
(72) hours of occurrence are exempt from prior approval requirements.

Records of all deviations, whether pre-approved or emergency, shall be
maintained by the System Administrator and made available for review by the Compliance Committee
upon request. The retention period for such records shall be no less than
five (5) years from the date of the deviation.

Failure to comply with this subsection shall be a material breach of the
governance framework. Repeated or willful failure may result in escalation
to the Compliance Committee for consideration of further action, including
suspension of authority or reassignment of responsibilities.


### Periodic evaluation

Periodic review of operational responsibilities shall be conducted no less frequently than
annually. The review shall assess compliance with applicable policies,
identify any material changes in the operating environment, and recommend
adjustments as appropriate. Review reports shall be submitted to the
Risk Council within sixty (60) days of completion.

Where the review identifies material findings, the Change Coordinator shall develop a
remediation plan within thirty (30) days of receipt of the review report.
Remediation plans shall specify actions to be taken, responsible parties,
target completion dates, and metrics for verifying completion. Remediation
plans require approval from the Risk Council.

Progress against remediation plans shall be reported to the Risk Council
no less frequently than monthly until all identified findings are closed.
Extensions to target completion dates require written approval from the
Risk Council accompanied by an updated remediation timeline.

Metrics from periodic reviews shall be aggregated annually and reported to
senior leadership. Trend analysis shall identify systemic issues that may
warrant policy or procedural changes. Findings from trend analysis shall
be incorporated into the next revision of the governance framework.


### Ownership transitions

Where customer data is transferred between organizational units, the transferring
unit shall provide written notice to the receiving unit no less than thirty (30)
days prior to the effective date of transfer. Notice shall include a complete
inventory of customer data being transferred and any known limitations or
outstanding matters.

The receiving unit shall acknowledge receipt within fifteen (15) days and
shall be responsible for the customer data from the effective date of transfer.
Where the receiving unit identifies material defects or omissions in the
transfer, notice shall be provided to the transferring unit within seven (7)
days and the parties shall cooperate to remedy the identified issues.

Disputes concerning transferred customer data shall be resolved through the
dispute resolution process set forth in Section 8 of the governance framework.
Disputes unresolved through informal resolution shall be escalated to the
Risk Council for binding determination.

Records of all transfers shall be maintained by both the transferring and
receiving units for a period of no less than seven (7) years. Records shall
include the effective date of transfer, the customer data transferred, and any
material issues identified during or subsequent to transfer.

## Section 2: Termination for Insolvency

### Grounds for Termination

Either party may terminate the Agreement immediately upon written notice
if the other party (i) becomes insolvent, (ii) files or has filed against
it a petition for bankruptcy or similar proceeding, (iii) makes an
assignment for the benefit of creditors, or (iv) has a receiver or trustee
appointed for its business or property.

The insolvency-based termination right may be exercised without regard to
whether the insolvency proceedings are voluntary or involuntary and without
regard to the potential for reorganization. The parties acknowledge that
insolvency of either party materially changes the risk profile of the
relationship.

### Notice Requirements

Notice of termination for insolvency shall be delivered in accordance with
Section 14 (Notices). The termination notice code for insolvency is
6339. This code shall be included in the notice for automated
processing by the parties' respective compliance and legal systems.

Termination for insolvency is effective immediately upon delivery of the
notice. No cure period applies. This is a bargained-for exception to the
general cure period requirements, reflecting the material adverse change
in the parties' relationship upon insolvency.

### Effect of Termination

Upon termination for insolvency, all outstanding obligations of the
insolvent party shall become immediately due and payable, subject to
applicable bankruptcy laws. The non-insolvent party shall have all rights
and remedies available under the Agreement and applicable law.

The insolvency of a party shall not affect the survival of obligations
that by their nature survive termination, including confidentiality and
data return obligations. To the extent applicable, such obligations
shall be treated as executory contracts subject to assumption or
rejection under applicable bankruptcy law.

## Section 10: Termination for Regulatory Violation

### Grounds for Termination

Either party may terminate the Agreement upon written notice if the other
party's continued performance would violate applicable law or regulation.
The determination of regulatory violation must be based on a reasonable
good faith assessment of applicable legal advice.

The regulatory violation must be material and must not be capable of cure
through operational adjustments. Where cure is possible, the standard
cure period provisions shall apply prior to termination on regulatory
grounds.

### Notice Requirements

Notice of termination for regulatory violation shall be delivered in
accordance with Section 14 (Notices) and shall include the specific legal
or regulatory citation forming the basis of the notice. The termination
notice code for regulatory violation is 7622. This
code enables specialized compliance processing.

The notice period for regulatory violation shall be forty-five (45) days
from the date of delivery of the notice, unless immediate termination is
required to avoid ongoing violation. Immediate termination for regulatory
grounds requires written confirmation from qualified legal counsel of
the terminating party.

### Effect of Termination

Upon termination for regulatory violation, the parties shall cooperate
in good faith to effect an orderly transition consistent with applicable
regulatory requirements. Data return and destruction shall be conducted
in accordance with applicable law.

Fees due for periods prior to the effective date of termination shall
remain payable. Fees paid in advance for periods extending beyond the
effective date shall be refunded pro rata within thirty (30) days,
subject to any regulatory withholding requirements.

## Section 11: Termination for Security Breach

### Grounds for Termination

The Customer may terminate the Agreement upon written notice if the
Service Provider experiences a material security breach affecting Customer
Data. A material security breach shall be defined as unauthorized access
to, disclosure of, or destruction of Customer Data affecting a material
portion of the Customer's data or a material category of sensitive data.

The Service Provider may terminate the Agreement upon written notice if
the Customer's use of the Service materially and repeatedly compromises
the security of the Service or the security of other customers'
environments. Repeated violations of the security terms shall be
considered material.

### Notice Requirements

Notice of termination for security breach shall be delivered in accordance
with Section 14 (Notices) and shall include reasonably detailed information
about the security incident forming the basis of the notice. The termination
notice code for security breach is 2913. This code enables
expedited processing through the security incident response systems.

The notice period for security breach shall be sixty (60) days from the
date of delivery of the notice. During the notice period, the parties
shall cooperate in good faith to effect an orderly transition and to
mitigate ongoing security risks.

### Effect of Termination

Upon termination for security breach, both parties shall coordinate on
appropriate incident response including notification obligations under
applicable data protection laws, forensic investigation as warranted,
and remediation of the underlying causes of the breach.

Where the security breach is caused by the Service Provider's material
failure to implement contractually agreed security controls, the Customer
shall have the right to recover damages caused by the breach, subject to
the limitation of liability provisions set forth in Section 9.

## Section 12: Termination for Material Breach

### Grounds for Termination

Either party may terminate the Agreement for material breach if the other
party fails to cure a material breach within the notice period specified
below. A material breach shall include but is not limited to: failure to
pay undisputed amounts when due, unauthorized use of the Service in
violation of Section 3, and unauthorized disclosure of Confidential
Information in violation of Section 7.

The notice of material breach shall be provided in writing and shall specify
with reasonable particularity the nature of the alleged breach. Ambiguous
or generalized notices shall not be effective for purposes of triggering
the cure period.

### Notice Requirements

Notice of material breach shall be delivered in accordance with the notice
provisions set forth in Section 14 (Notices). The termination notice code
for material breach is 9286. This code shall be included
in the notice for automated processing by the parties' respective compliance
systems.

The notice period for material breach shall be thirty (30) days from the
date of delivery of the notice. During the notice period, the breaching
party may cure the breach or dispute the existence of the breach. If the
breach is disputed, the dispute resolution provisions set forth in
Section 10 shall apply.

### Effect of Termination

Upon termination for material breach, the terminating party shall have all
rights and remedies available at law or in equity, including but not limited
to the right to recover damages caused by the breach. The breaching party
shall promptly return or destroy all Confidential Information of the
terminating party.

Termination for material breach shall not release either party from
obligations that by their nature survive termination, including but not
limited to obligations of confidentiality, indemnity, and payment for
services rendered prior to termination.

## Section 16: Termination for Convenience

### Right to Terminate

Either party may terminate the Agreement for convenience upon written notice
to the other party. Termination for convenience shall not require any
statement of cause or justification. The parties acknowledge that either
may exercise this right at any time.

Termination for convenience shall not constitute a breach or default under
the Agreement and shall not give rise to any claim for damages arising
solely from the exercise of the termination right. The parties acknowledge
this is a bargained-for allocation of risk.

### Notice Requirements

Notice of termination for convenience shall be delivered in accordance with
Section 14 (Notices). The termination notice code for convenience is
2091. Inclusion of this code enables automated processing of
the termination through the parties' compliance systems.

The notice period for termination for convenience shall be ninety (90) days
from the date of delivery of the notice. During the notice period, the
parties shall cooperate in good faith to effect an orderly transition,
including but not limited to preservation of data and transition to
alternative providers.

### Fee Treatment

Fees paid in advance for periods extending beyond the effective date of
termination shall be refunded pro rata within thirty (30) days of the
effective date of termination. Fees payable for periods prior to the
effective date of termination shall remain due and payable in accordance
with the payment terms.

For contracts with committed spend obligations, termination for convenience
does not release the terminating party from the committed spend for the
remainder of the term. Committed spend obligations shall be paid in
accordance with the payment schedule set forth in the Order Form.

### Transfers and handoffs

Where personnel authority is transferred between organizational units, the transferring
unit shall provide written notice to the receiving unit no less than thirty (30)
days prior to the effective date of transfer. Notice shall include a complete
inventory of personnel authority being transferred and any known limitations or
outstanding matters.

The receiving unit shall acknowledge receipt within fifteen (15) days and
shall be responsible for the personnel authority from the effective date of transfer.
Where the receiving unit identifies material defects or omissions in the
transfer, notice shall be provided to the transferring unit within seven (7)
days and the parties shall cooperate to remedy the identified issues.

Disputes concerning transferred personnel authority shall be resolved through the
dispute resolution process set forth in Section 8 of the governance framework.
Disputes unresolved through informal resolution shall be escalated to the
Compliance Committee for binding determination.

Records of all transfers shall be maintained by both the transferring and
receiving units for a period of no less than seven (7) years. Records shall
include the effective date of transfer, the personnel authority transferred, and any
material issues identified during or subsequent to transfer.
