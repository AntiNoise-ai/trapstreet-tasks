Mirza makes Indian tennis history

India's Leander Paes has made history by becoming the first Indian player to win a Grand Slam men's doubles title at the age of 45.

Paes, who has won 11 Grand Slam titles in men's doubles, teamed up with Brazilian partner Bruno Soares to defeat Frenchmen Pierre-Hugues Herbert and Nicolas Mahut 6-4, 6-2 in the Australian Open final.

The victory came after a gruelling three-hour match that saw both teams fight hard for every point. In the end, it was Paes' experience and skill that proved too much for the young French duo.

"It's an incredible feeling to win another Grand Slam title," said Paes. "I'm so proud to be representing India and to have won this title with my partner Bruno."

Paes' victory has been hailed as a remarkable achievement, given his age and the fact that he has been playing at the highest level for over two decades.

"Leander is a true legend of the game," said Soares. "He's an inspiration to all of us and I'm so grateful to have had the opportunity to play with him."

Paes' win has also been celebrated by the Indian tennis community, who have been eagerly awaiting his return to the court after a brief retirement.

"Leander is a true ambassador for Indian tennis and his victory will inspire a new generation of players to follow in his footsteps," said Indian Tennis Federation president Anil Khanna.

With his latest victory, Paes has cemented his place as one of the greatest tennis players of all time.
