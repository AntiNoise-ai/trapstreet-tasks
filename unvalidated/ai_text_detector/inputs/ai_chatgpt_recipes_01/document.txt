Ingredients:
- 1 pound of cooked prawns, peeled and deveined
- 1 iceberg lettuce, shredded
- 1 small cucumber, diced
- 1 small red onion, finely chopped
- 1 avocado, diced
- 1 lemon, juiced
- 1/2 cup of mayonnaise
- 2 tablespoons of ketchup
- 1 tablespoon of Worcestershire sauce
- 1 teaspoon of horseradish sauce
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Fresh dill or parsley for garnish

Instructions:
1. In a large bowl, combine the shredded lettuce, diced cucumber, chopped red onion, and diced avocado. Toss gently to mix well.
2. In a separate bowl, mix together the mayonnaise, ketchup, Worcestershire sauce, horseradish sauce, and lemon juice. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
3. Add the cooked prawns to the dressing mixture and stir gently to coat the prawns evenly.
4. Divide the lettuce mixture among serving plates or bowls.
5. Spoon the prawn mixture on top of the lettuce.
6. Garnish with fresh dill or parsley.
7. Serve immediately and enjoy your delicious Prawn Cocktail A La Delia Smith!
