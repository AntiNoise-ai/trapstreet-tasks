This classic starter recipe is from Delia Smith.

Ingredients:

* 300g shelled medium prawns (allow about 20 per person)
* 1 tablespoon lemon juice
* 4 tablespoons mayonnaise
* 1 teaspoon smooth mustard
* 1 teaspoon tomato ketchup
* ½ teaspoon Worcestershire sauce
* a good grinding of black pepper
* a little salt
* 2 gem lettuces
* 1 lemon, cut into wedges
* 1 bunch of watercress

Method:

* 1) Remove the heads and shells from the prawns, but leave the tails on.
* 2) Slice the lettuce leaves into ribbons and place in the base of 6 small glasses or bowls.
* 3) Mix together the lemon juice, mayonnaise, mustard, ketchup, Worcestershire sauce and pepper.
* 4) Stir in the prawns, then pile on top of the lettuce.
* 5) Sprinkle with a little salt and decorate each glass with a wedge of lemon and a sprig of watercress.
