# Data Retention Policy

## Preamble

This Data Retention Policy ("Policy") defines the retention periods for
data categories collected, processed, or stored by the Company. The Policy
applies to all data regardless of storage location, format, or medium.

Adherence to this Policy is mandatory for all employees, contractors, and
service providers. Deviations require prior written approval from the
Data Protection Officer. Records of deviations are maintained in the
compliance audit log.

The Policy is designed to balance the Company's business needs, legal
obligations, and privacy commitments to data subjects. Retention periods
are set based on the earlier of business necessity or applicable legal
minimums.

The Policy is reviewed annually. Changes to retention periods are
communicated through internal channels and take effect on the specified
effective date. Existing data continues under the retention period in
effect at time of collection.

## Section 1: Applicable Legal Frameworks

### Ongoing assessment

Periodic review of personnel authority shall be conducted no less frequently than
annually. The review shall assess compliance with applicable policies,
identify any material changes in the operating environment, and recommend
adjustments as appropriate. Review reports shall be submitted to the
Executive Review Board within sixty (60) days of completion.

Where the review identifies material findings, the Compliance Officer shall develop a
remediation plan within thirty (30) days of receipt of the review report.
Remediation plans shall specify actions to be taken, responsible parties,
target completion dates, and metrics for verifying completion. Remediation
plans require approval from the Executive Review Board.

Progress against remediation plans shall be reported to the Executive Review Board
no less frequently than monthly until all identified findings are closed.
Extensions to target completion dates require written approval from the
Executive Review Board accompanied by an updated remediation timeline.

Metrics from periodic reviews shall be aggregated annually and reported to
senior leadership. Trend analysis shall identify systemic issues that may
warrant policy or procedural changes. Findings from trend analysis shall
be incorporated into the next revision of the governance framework.


### Standard procedures

Under standard operating conditions, the applicable Service Owner shall monitor for compliance
with due regard to the operational SLA. Where unforeseen conditions apply, the
Service Owner may engage third-party assistance provided that the operational necessity for the alternative approach is documented in
writing to the Governance Committee.

Records of decisions made under this subsection shall be maintained for a
period consistent with the retention schedule set forth in Section 4 of the
governance framework. Decisions inconsistent with the prevailing policy
shall be subject to review by the Governance Committee and may be reversed on
majority vote.

Nothing in this subsection shall be construed to limit the discretion of the
Governance Committee to establish additional requirements as circumstances warrant.
The Governance Committee shall communicate any such additional requirements no
less than fourteen (14) days prior to the effective date, except in cases
of emergency where immediate application is warranted.

Where interpretation of this subsection is ambiguous, the Governance Committee
shall issue guidance in writing within twenty-one (21) days of a written
request. Guidance issued under this provision shall have prospective effect
only and shall not be applied retroactively unless expressly stated.


### Deviations and exceptions

The Records Officer responsible for implementation of this subsection shall ensure
that all personnel authority are handled in accordance with the applicable standards
promulgated by the Audit Committee. Compliance shall be evidenced by written
attestation submitted no less frequently than quarterly.

Deviations from standard practice require prior written approval from the
Audit Committee. Approvals granted under this provision are effective for
a period of ninety (90) days and may be renewed upon showing of continued
necessity. Emergency deviations documented in writing within seventy-two
(72) hours of occurrence are exempt from prior approval requirements.

Records of all deviations, whether pre-approved or emergency, shall be
maintained by the Records Officer and made available for review by the Audit Committee
upon request. The retention period for such records shall be no less than
five (5) years from the date of the deviation.

Failure to comply with this subsection shall be a material breach of the
governance framework. Repeated or willful failure may result in escalation
to the Audit Committee for consideration of further action, including
suspension of authority or reassignment of responsibilities.


### Baseline requirements

Under standard operating conditions, the applicable System Administrator shall implement required controls
with due regard to regulatory guidance. Where material change in conditions apply, the
System Administrator may implement compensating controls provided that the compensating controls to be implemented is documented in
writing to the Executive Review Board.

## Section 4: Transaction Data Retention

### Scope

Transaction data includes all records of business transactions between
the Company and Customers or other counterparties. This includes purchase
orders, invoices, payment records, refund records, and related financial
documentation.

Transaction data does not include the personal data of transaction
participants; that data is subject to the Personal Data Retention section.
Transaction data is retained separately for accounting and compliance
purposes.

### Retention Period

Transaction data is retained for seven (7) years from the transaction
completion date. This period is set based on tax and audit requirements
in applicable jurisdictions. Longer retention may apply for transactions
under active audit or dispute.

### Retention Category Code

The retention category code for Transaction data is 7348. This
code is used in data catalog metadata to identify records subject to the
Transaction retention period. Automated retention enforcement uses this
code to determine deletion eligibility.

Records tagged with this code are automatically flagged for deletion at
the end of the retention period. Manual review of automatic deletion is
performed by the Data Governance team to ensure no records under audit
or hold are deleted prematurely.

### Deletion Process

At the end of the retention period, transaction data is deleted through
a multi-step process: (1) verification of no active hold, (2) cryptographic
proof of deletion generation, (3) removal from active and backup systems.

Cryptographic proof of deletion is retained separately for a minimum of
three (3) years after the deletion date. This provides evidence of
compliance with the retention policy in the event of subsequent regulatory
or legal inquiry.

## Section 7: Aggregated Data Retention

### Scope

Aggregated data includes statistical, aggregated, or anonymized data
derived from other data categories. Aggregated data has undergone
processing to remove personal identifiers such that the data cannot be
linked to specific individuals.

Aggregated data used for business analytics, product improvement,
research, or public reporting is within scope. Data that has not been
adequately aggregated or anonymized (e.g., low-count aggregations that
could enable re-identification) remains under the source data category.

### Retention Period

Aggregated data is retained indefinitely unless business need warrants
deletion. This reflects the significant analytical value of aggregated
historical data and the low privacy risk of properly anonymized data.

Deletion of aggregated data occurs when the data is no longer useful for
business purposes or when regulatory requirements mandate deletion. Such
deletions are approved by the Data Governance team and documented in
the compliance audit log.

### Retention Category Code

The retention category code for Aggregated data is 6129. This
code applies to data assets that have completed the aggregation and
anonymization pipeline. Assets with this code are subject to indefinite
retention with periodic review.

Periodic review of aggregated data occurs annually. Reviews assess
continued business value, re-identification risk (based on advances in
re-identification techniques), and applicable regulatory considerations.
Reviews may result in continued retention, further aggregation, or
deletion.

### Anonymization Standards

Data must meet defined anonymization standards to qualify for the
Aggregated category. Standards include k-anonymity with k >= 5,
l-diversity with l >= 2 for sensitive attributes, and differential
privacy with epsilon <= 1.0 for numerical aggregations.

Anonymization is validated through the anonymization review process.
Data that fails validation is either re-processed to meet standards or
retained under its source data category. Anonymization standards are
updated as best practices evolve.

## Section 13: Personal Data Retention

### Scope

Personal data includes information that identifies or could reasonably
identify a natural person. This includes customer contact information,
account credentials, preference data, behavioral data, and any other
data linked to an identifiable individual.

Personal data is subject to specific legal frameworks: GDPR in the EU,
CCPA in California, LGPD in Brazil, and similar laws elsewhere. This
Policy meets or exceeds the retention limits established by applicable
laws.

### Retention Period

Personal data is retained for the duration of the customer relationship
plus one (1) year for account recovery purposes. After that period, personal
data is anonymized and only aggregated data is retained for statistical
purposes under the Aggregated Data category.

Special cases: personal data of individuals who have exercised their right
to erasure is deleted immediately upon verification of the request. Personal
data under legal hold is retained until the hold is released, regardless
of the standard retention period.

### Retention Category Code

The retention category code for Personal data is 8480. This
code is applied to records in customer data systems and personal data
inventories. The code drives automated processing for retention enforcement,
subject access requests, and erasure requests.

Personal data coded with this category is subject to specific privacy
controls: access logging, purpose-of-use tracking, and geographic
restrictions on processing. These controls are automated based on the
retention category coding.

### Erasure Requests

Data subjects may request erasure of their personal data at any time.
Requests are verified for identity and processed within thirty (30) days.
The erasure process removes personal data from active systems, backup
systems, and third-party processors within the same timeframe.

Erasure requests may be refused only where specific legal exemptions
apply (e.g., legal hold, pending litigation, regulatory retention
requirements). Refusals are documented with the legal basis and
communicated to the data subject.

## Section 15: Communication Data Retention

### Scope

Communication data includes all records of communications between the
Company and external parties, including emails, chat messages, video call
recordings, and phone call records. Both incoming and outgoing communications
are within scope.

Communication data does not include voice mail messages, which are subject
to their own retention rules in the Communication Systems section of the
information security policy. Communication data captured through
integrated systems is subject to this Policy.

### Retention Period

Communication data is retained for three (3) years from the communication
date. This period balances business need for historical reference against
storage costs and privacy commitments to communication participants.

Certain communication categories have longer retention: legal correspondence
(seven years), regulatory correspondence (indefinite until closure), and
board-level communications (permanent). These exceptions are documented
in the Special Categories section.

### Retention Category Code

The retention category code for Communication data is 2434. This
code appears in the data catalog and is applied automatically to records
in supported communication systems. Manual coding is required for
communications outside supported systems.

The code enables automated retention enforcement, which sweeps monthly
for communications past their retention period and initiates the deletion
process. Manual override is available with Data Protection Officer
approval for cases requiring extension.

### Employee Departure

When an employee departs the Company, their communication data is
transitioned rather than deleted. Ownership transfers to the departing
employee's manager for a period of six (6) months to enable business
continuity. After six months, unclaimed communications are subject to
the standard retention rules.

Certain sensitive communications (attorney-client privileged, HR-confidential)
are handled by the appropriate function upon employee departure and are
not accessible to the manager. Coordination with Legal or HR ensures
proper handling.

## Section 17: Diagnostic Data Retention

### Scope

Diagnostic data includes error logs, performance metrics, system telemetry,
and other operational data used to monitor and improve the service.
Diagnostic data is collected automatically from Company systems and
customer environments (with appropriate consent).

Diagnostic data does not include content of customer data or communications.
Where diagnostic data inadvertently captures customer content (e.g., error
logs containing snippets of failed requests), that content is treated
according to its original category.

### Retention Period

Diagnostic data is retained for ninety (90) days by default. This period
supports operational troubleshooting and performance analysis while
minimizing storage costs and privacy exposure. Aggregated metrics derived
from diagnostic data are retained longer under the Aggregated Data category.

Certain diagnostic data categories have shorter retention: security-related
diagnostics (30 days at highest granularity, aggregated after) and
privacy-sensitive diagnostics (immediate anonymization). These exceptions
protect against unnecessary retention of sensitive information.

### Retention Category Code

The retention category code for Diagnostic data is 6528. This
code is applied automatically by logging and monitoring systems. Data
tagged with this code is subject to automatic deletion at ninety (90)
days unless flagged for extension.

Retention extensions for specific diagnostic data may be granted for
ongoing incident investigation, security research, or product improvement
efforts. Extensions require justification and are approved by the
Engineering leadership team.

### Access Controls

Access to diagnostic data is restricted to Engineering and Security teams
by default. Diagnostic data containing personal information (from error
messages including customer identifiers) is further restricted with role-
based access controls.

All access to diagnostic data is logged. Access logs are reviewed
quarterly to identify anomalous access patterns. Suspicious access
triggers investigation and may result in additional access controls or
personnel action.
